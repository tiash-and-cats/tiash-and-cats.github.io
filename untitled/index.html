A "nice" bitmap font face that looks cool.

Features:

  • Bitmap (5x5)
  • "Mixed unicase":
    • Uppercase and lowercase share a glyph set.
    • Each glyph was chosen from the uppercase or lowercase form based on readability in 5x5 pixels.
  • Designed in around 1hr
  • Does not have @, ~, |, or `
  • Does not have an italic variant
  • Licensed under the OFL

How does it look? You're looking at it right now!

Not to be confused with the Untitled Pixel Font by Shane Hernandez, which is also quite cool. It is under a less permissive license which does not permit redistribution, though, so be careful.
